    Who it's for

    Built for the people who keep fleets moving.

    One place for what is happening on the road, who is dealing with it, and what to fix so it happens less.

    • Dispatchers

      A live queue of incidents across every fleet. Take one, instruct or call the driver, and close it with the outcome. Nothing falls through the cracks.

    • Fleet managers

      Your vehicles and drivers only. See which drivers need coaching and which vehicles need a check, and book it from the same screen.

    • Operations leads

      Incidents per 100 km, how fast the control room responds, where incidents cluster, and how each fleet compares.
